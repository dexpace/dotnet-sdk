# Phase 8a — The Conformance Kit: Checklist

The execution-time checklist for sub-phase 8a of roadmap phase 8
([phase card](../../2026-09-27-dotnet-sdk-v1-roadmap-design.md)): one row per requirement ID, in the legend of the roadmap's
cross-cutting constraint 3. Written from what was built on branch `88-phase-8a-conformance-kit` (issue #88), following the
[design](2026-10-09-phase8a-conformance-kit-design.md) and the [plan](2026-10-09-phase8a-conformance-kit.md). User page:
[`conformance.md`](../../../../sdk-documentation/conformance.md).

The scope is 38 rows, `TRANSPORT-2`, `-3`, `-7`, `-11`, `-15`, `-16`, `-19`, `-20`, `-21`, `-22`, `-23`, `-24`, `-25`, `-26`, `-27`, `-29` and `ASYNC-1` to `ASYNC-22`
(32 MUST, 6 SHOULD): **33 ✅, 1 ✅ with one clause ⏳ (`ASYNC-2`, finding F-A2 below), 1 ⏳ 8b (`TRANSPORT-2`, finding F-T2 below), 3 N/A or vacuous (`ASYNC-4`, `ASYNC-16`, `ASYNC-21`)**, 0 rows
wholly ⏳ with no owner; `ASYNC-6` is ✅ on the bridges with its runtime-facade half vacuous. The 14 rows phase 8b owns are listed as ⏳ 8b with their assertion names
[below](#the-14-rows-handed-to-phase-8b), and `TRANSPORT-2` joins them as a carried clause (the census stays 38 + 14 = 52); 8a wrote **all 43 assertions**, including theirs. The package is `Dexpace.Sdk.Conformance`; 8a changes **no line** of
`src/Dexpace.Sdk.Http.SystemNet` or `src/Dexpace.Sdk.Core` and adds **no** `Security` class (the phase-1 classes are `using`-swapped by one line and otherwise
unedited).

**Adaptation to the as-built code.** The plan's three groups landed as commits on the one branch, in the plan's order: the package, the report machinery, the
catalogue generator and the promoted fixture (group 1); the subject and runner, the raw-socket client, the assertions with their negative controls, the two
drivers and the AOT slice (group 2); the `ASYNC` pins, the documentation and the close-out (group 3). The deviations from the plan are listed
[below](#deviations-from-the-plan).

**Red evidence.** Every new type's first compile was red (a missing type or member). The negative control of every assertion is a deliberately broken subject
that must yield `Failed`; the 12 `*ControlTests` classes hold 79 of them and an architecture-style pin (`AssertionCatalogueTests`) fails if an assertion has none,
found by shape without any test having run. Mutations performed and reverted (never committed): the fixture's second-request release (the observation test failed),
the gate's one-byte watch read left pending (it ate a byte of the next request: the keep-alive-after-a-gate test failed once strengthened to check the recorded
request line), the runner's stale-waiver conversion, face narrowing and cancellation disposal (four runner tests failed), and in core the post-`Execute`
cancellation check (the `ASYNC-7` pin and two existing tests failed) and `ExecutionContext` flow into the worker (the `ASYNC-8` and `ASYNC-10` pins and two
existing tests failed). `ASYNC-9` pins what the runtime itself restores: no bridge mutation can fail it. `ASYNC-11`'s clearing half was first pinned on a thread with no context of
its own, which no mutation could fail; the review replaced it with a worker that has a scope of its own, and the same `ExecutionContext` mutation now fails it (with the `ASYNC-8` and `ASYNC-10` pins).
After the review, each assertion it touched was also run against a mutated subject: the old request-count script of `transport-20.retried-by-the-pipeline` with the new attempt count fails on the reference
transport ("actual: 1"), a transport that retries a body-less request fails `transport-2.bodyless-not-resent`, and every new control fails with its clause's own message, not a hang.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **43 assertions, not 40.** `transport-14.inbound-lenient` is three assertions (`value-control-dropped`, `obs-text-preserved`, `name-malformed-dropped`), the amendment the 8b design
   asked of 8a (its P8b-14), so 8b can waive the one clause the native client cannot meet. The plan's R3 (`ConformanceWaiver.Assertion` and `.Face`) was already the other half. The 43rd is
   `transport-2.bodyless-not-resent`, added by the review of the phase (deviation 14).
2. **R1.** Appendix B.7 has six checklist items, not five: `ByAppendixBItem` has 11 keys, `B.6.1` to `B.7.6`, checked against the appendix by a drift test.
3. **R3, R4, R5, R13 taken as planned.** The waiver narrows by assertion and face; the raw-socket driver waives only `TRANSPORT-11`'s logging clause (it waived `TRANSPORT-13` too until the review made that assertion measure its own antecedent); an assertion's level is declared;
   the fixture records `ServerClosedFirst` and `Status`, `Ok`, `Large` and `Streamed` take `keepAlive`.
4. **Releasing is observed by reuse as well as by close, with a probe burst.** The plan's `Check.ReleasedAsync` waited for the server to see a close or a further request; a pooling client returns a released
   connection to its pool and does neither, so the check sends concurrent probe requests through the transport until the connection shows up (a pool reuses its newest idle connection first, so one
   sequential probe is not enough). A connection a leaked response still holds is never shown released, which is the point. Release checks that need a fresh connection each (`transport-9`, `-24`) use a fresh server per round.
5. **A transport failure where the clause requires success is `Failed`, not `Errored`** (`Check.GuardAsync`, `ExpectResponseAsync`, `ReadBodyAsync`): `Errored` is for kit bugs.
6. **`seam-15.after-dispose` also exercises the borrowed transport** when the subject supplies `CreateBorrowed`, so the reference transport fails it where the design said it would (the owned form already throws).
7. **`transport-13` reads "loudly" as Warning or above and "quietly" as anything lower**, the reading 8b's design gives its default policy; **`transport-18` has two halves** (replay identical bytes; fail with an `SdkException` when buffering the body fails).
8. **No `RawSocketResponseBody` class**: the raw client returns core's `ResponseBody.FromStream` over a framing stream that owns the socket.
9. **Two additions that change no public surface.** `ConformanceReport.Create` has an internal overload that takes the configured waivers, so every waiver is listed on every report even when none applied; and the suite derives a three-second `TimeoutBound` (or half the assertion bound when that is shorter) for the assertions about calls a timeout should cut, so a transport that ignores `RequestOptions.Timeout` fails named and fast instead of at the assertion's full bound.
10. **The SystemNet driver pins `HttpClient.DefaultProxy` to direct** in a static constructor: the owned `SystemNetHttpClient(ILogger)` honours `HTTP_PROXY`, and the internal constructor that pins `UseProxy = false` takes no logger.
11. **Tasks 1.11 and 1.12 (and 2.16) produced gates, not commits of their own**, apart from the CI header (`ci:`), whose edit also rewords the NativeAOT line of the "wired but inert" list (it already said the smoke consumer runs).
12. **ASYNC pins:** `The_call_runs_on_the_given_scheduler` already existed, so no scheduler pin was added; `ASYNC-9` and `ASYNC-11` cannot be mutated away (above). **F-A2** stands as the plan wrote it and was re-measured here.
13. **One unexplained transient failure** of the core suite (1 of 5357) was seen once, straight after a mutation rebuild, and not reproduced in seven further runs of the suite; it is not in a test this phase added that could be identified, and is recorded rather than explained.
    The review found a race in a test this phase did add and did not claim it as the cause: `The_workers_own_scope_is_intact_after_a_call_returns_and_after_one_throws` read the worker's record right after the last
    awaited call returned, while the worker thread could still be about to write that record. It now reads after the worker is disposed (joined). Whether that was the transient failure is not known.
14. **Review corrections** (seven verified findings, each fixed with its own commit; the kit's behaviour changed only where a clause was not actually tested):
    - **F-T2, `TRANSPORT-2` closed as met while the native client re-sends silently (blocker).** The row moved from ✅ to ⏳ 8b (above); a 43rd assertion, `transport-2.bodyless-not-resent`, with a control, a raw-socket pass and an `Owner = "8b"` waiver on the reference transport; fact F6 corrected (design census, knowledge note).
    - **`transport-20.retried-by-the-pipeline` never exercised the pipeline's retry on the reference transport (major).** The server now answers by the pipeline's attempt number and a counting wrapper requires at least two attempts; fact F6's "once, two aborts surface as `HttpRequestException`" was wrong (up to three more sends, on new connections too).
    - **`transport-13` could not pass anywhere and reported `Failed` for a transport that drops nothing (major).** It is now `Vacuous` when no name was dropped (measured from the server's side); the raw-socket waiver is deleted; a test-side dropper shows it `Passed`; a control for a silent dropper was added.
    - **`transport-3` accepted a cancellation without the caller's token (minor)** and **`transport-7.attempt-timeout-aborts` accepted any `SdkException` (minor).** Both now require the thing their clause names (the caller's token on the exception or its cause chain; a timeout on the failure, its causes or its trail), each with new controls.
    - **The `ASYNC-11` pin could not fail (minor).** Replaced by a worker-with-its-own-scope pin that the `ExecutionContext` mutation fails (above).
    - **`LoopbackServer`'s remarks still said it will be promoted (minor).** They state the library contract (BCL only, no test-framework types, every await configured, observation members safe from any thread).

## Verdicts for the lead

- **D3 is confirmed** (P8a-2, still for the lead to rule): no `TRANSPORT` or `ASYNC` property was unexercisable through `SystemNetHttpClient`; the kit runs green against a second, independent wire stack (the raw-socket client through
  `DelegateHttpClient`) and against the reference transport, with a documented waiver per gap. Building a second first-party transport is not warranted.
- **`Dexpace.Sdk.Reactive` is not built** (P8a-3): no consumer found.
- **First published version of the kit: `0.1.0` is proposed** (P8a-5), the first release's lockstep version; `docs/first-release.md` makes the number phase 12's decision.
- **F-A2 (`ASYNC-2`'s worker-pool-rejection clause) needs a ruling:** accept ⏳ (the default here) or admit a one-method core change (`SyncToAsyncAdapter.ExecuteAsync` wraps `StartNew` and returns `Task.FromException`).
- **F-T2 (`TRANSPORT-2`) needs a disposition, and is a design question:** `SocketsHttpHandler` re-sends a body-less request reset before any response byte, and exposes no switch. Either 8b finds a handler-level way to turn the retry off
  (then it deletes the waiver), or the MUST is recorded as unmet on a stated domain (a design §10 entry, an *Unsatisfied MUSTs* line, a permanent waiver). The earlier informal "(8b)" label on `TRANSPORT-2` in the 6a checklist was right and this
  phase's reassignment of the row to 8a (P8a-1) was wrong for this one row; the other three reassigned rows (`-11`, `-22`, `-27`) stand.
