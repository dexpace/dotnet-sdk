## Conflicts
- **Public interfaces keep the `I` prefix.** Resolves `naming-conventions/0a617faa` in favour of the SDK and overrides `naming-conventions/a3f2e504`, `naming-conventions/ce2cee56`, `naming-conventions/2abd8ffa`, `naming-conventions/79da3386` and `naming-conventions/b97bbbd8` (the `IDE1006` analyzer that requires the prefix stays on) for the public API of every `src/` project: `IHttpClient`, `IAsyncHttpClient` and `ISerde` keep their names, because the Framework Design Guidelines are the stronger authority for a public NuGet surface and a prefix-less `HttpClient` would collide with `System.Net.Http.HttpClient` in every consumer's scope. The departure is the SDK overlay's recorded row, design section 10 entry 27, and roadmap constraint 6 (phase 0 decision: kept). Everything else in the rule stands, including that BCL interfaces keep their `I`. It likewise overrides `naming-conventions/37eed2e7`, the chapter's own summary of the two deviations, which describes the guide's house style, not this SDK's public surface.
